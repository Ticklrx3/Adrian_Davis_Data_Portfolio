How I work

A disciplined path from raw records to useful insight

Strong analysis is not just a chart at the end. It starts with a clearly defined question and a trustworthy dataset, then finishes with a result the audience can act on.

  1. 01
    Frame the question

    Clarify the decision, audience, definitions, and success criteria.

  2. 02
    Prepare and validate

    Standardize inputs, reconcile sources, investigate anomalies, and document assumptions.

  3. 03
    Analyze and test

    Compare trends, calculate KPIs, test logic, and verify that results match the source.

  4. 04
    Communicate clearly

    Build concise dashboards and recommendations matched to the stakeholder’s needs.
